# GeoFS Failures
GeoFS Failures introduces an element of unpredictability and realism to your flight experience by simulating various mechanical and systemic failures. You can trigger failures manually or set probability "chances" for systems to fail spontaneously during flight.
## Installation
Step 1: Copy the contents of 'userscript.js' into your clipboard  
Step 2: If you haven't already, download Tampermonkey (https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/tampermonkey/dhdgffkkebhmkfjojejmpbldmpobfkfo//Open)  
Step 3: Press the Extensions icon on the toolbar at the top of your browser and click on the icon that looks like a rounded square with two eyes at the bottom, and press "Dashboard"  
Step 4: Press the little "+" tab, delete everything in the big text box, paste your clipboard contents into that text box, and press Ctrl/Cmd + S to save it.  
Step 5: Open GeoFS, and it should work! 

Note: When installing Tampermonkey, you may need to enable Developer Mode inside of the browser's settings, and click "Allow User Scripts" in Tampermonkey's browser settings.

## Usage
**Opening the Menu**

Once the game loads, you will see a black FAILURES button on the right side of your screen. Click it to toggle the control panel.  

**Enabling Random Failures**  

By default, failures will not happen automatically. To enable probability-based failures, open the Failures menu, adjust the "Chance per minute" sliders for the systems you want to be "unreliable." (1.00 = 100% chance every minute, 0.01 = 1% chance; realistic values are extremely low). Click the "Enable" button at the top of the menu.  

**Manual Failures**

If you want to practice a specific emergency, simply find the system in the menu and click the "FAIL" button next to it. An alert will notify you of the failure.  

**Resetting**  

In real life, there is no reset button. In this addon, if you want to restore your aircraft to perfect condition, click "**RESET ALL**". Note that for some structural or visual failures, a page refresh may be required to fully reset the aircraft model.
